Buddies Spa LLC ("Buddies Spa", "we") respects the privacy of people who visit this website or contact us through it. This policy explains what the website collects, why, and the service providers that process it on our behalf.

Information we collect

When you use a form on this website, you may give us your name, email address, phone number and your message. Depending on the form, you may also share:

  • Job applications: the position you're applying for, license details, a portfolio or social media link, and your availability.
  • Private event inquiries: event type, preferred date, guest count, services wanted and budget range.
  • Partnership inquiries: your business name, website, business type and partnership interests.

The website and its hosting providers also process routine technical information needed to deliver and secure the site, such as IP address, browser and device information, and security logs.

How information is used

We use what you send us to reply to you, consider job applications, plan events, evaluate partnerships, keep appropriate business records, prevent abuse, and protect the website and its visitors. We do not sell personal information.

Form submissions are emailed directly to our team. They are not stored in a database on this website.

Booking, payments and gift cards

Appointments, payments and gift cards are handled by Square on Square's own pages. Information you give Square is governed by Square's privacy policy, not this one.

Service providers

The website is hosted, and its forms are processed, by Netlify. Form messages are delivered by email through Resend. Google reviews shown on the home page are retrieved from Google by our server; your browser does not connect to Google to display them. Fonts and other site files are served directly from this website, and no third-party analytics or advertising services are loaded.

Retention and security

We keep form submissions only as long as reasonably needed to respond, run our business, meet legal obligations and keep things secure. We use reasonable safeguards, but no internet transmission or storage system is completely secure.

Cookies

This website does not use advertising cookies or behavioral tracking. Our infrastructure providers may process standard request information as part of normal network and security operations.

External links

This website links to Square, social media, partner websites and event platforms such as Partiful. We are not responsible for their privacy practices; please review their policies.

Questions and requests

To ask about information you sent through this website, request a correction, or ask us to delete it, email buddiesmensspa@gmail.com or call (202) 926-7178. Some information may need to be kept for legitimate business or legal reasons.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when the website, our providers or our practices change. The current version, with its date, is always on this page.
