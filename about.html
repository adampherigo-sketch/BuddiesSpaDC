Our story

Where self-care meets inclusivity.

Buddies Spa is a full-service wellness and beauty destination in the heart of Mount Vernon Triangle, built so that every client feels respected, valued and empowered.

A vision of inclusivity

Founded with pride in 2022.

Buddies was founded by Kris Otto, an Asian immigrant and proud member of the LGBTQAI+ community, out of a passion for self-care, diversity and empowerment.

Kris began in the beauty industry as a nail technician before moving into waxing, and saw how much people needed a spa where individuals of every identity and background could feel truly comfortable and celebrated.

That vision shaped Buddies into more than a beauty and wellness center. It's a sanctuary where clients can relax, rejuvenate and thrive.
